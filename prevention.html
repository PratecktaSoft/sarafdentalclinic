Preventive Dentistry

THE BEST TREATMENT IS THE ONE AVOIDED.

Structured hygiene and screening designed to catch problems early — before they become procedures.

Introduction

Consistent, unglamorous care that prevents the need for bigger interventions.

Biannual hygiene visits, periodontal screening and early cavity detection form the foundation of long-term oral health — and the foundation of every other treatment we provide.

Why It Matters

Most restorative dentistry exists because something preventable went undetected. Structured screening catches issues while they're still simple to treat.

Technology

Intraoral cameras and digital X-rays let us detect early-stage issues that are invisible to the naked eye, often years before symptoms appear.

Benefits

What patients gain

Fewer, less invasive procedures over your lifetime.

Lower long-term treatment costs.

Early detection of periodontal disease.

A clear, personalized maintenance schedule.
