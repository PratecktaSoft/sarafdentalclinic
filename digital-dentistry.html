11 / QUESTIONS

DIGITAL FAQ.

01 Is CBCT imaging safe?

+

CBCT is used when clinically justified. Your dental team determines whether three-dimensional imaging is appropriate for your specific case.

02 Do I need a physical impression?

+

Digital scanning can replace traditional impressions for many types of dental treatment.

03 Can I see my proposed result?

+

For suitable aesthetic cases, digital visualization can be used during the planning and discussion stage.

04 Is every treatment fully digital?

+

No. Digital tools are selected according to the clinical requirements of each case.
