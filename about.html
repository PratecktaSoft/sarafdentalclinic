Saraf Dental Care

BUILT AROUND PRECISION.

Founded by Dr. Shakun Saraf (BDS, MDS), Saraf Dental Care has grown into one of Akola's trusted multi-specialty dental practices.

Our approach combines clinical precision with a calm, comfortable experience for every patient.

02 / OUR APPROACH

A decade of narrowing our focus — not widening it.

Rather than becoming a general practice, Saraf Dental Care has spent ten years going deeper into implantology, prosthodontics and digital planning — the disciplines where precision compounds into better long-term outcomes.

Precision becomes meaningful when it improves a life.
